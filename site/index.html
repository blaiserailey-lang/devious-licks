A console on your Mac

The library is a dashboard you drive with a controller, the keyboard or the mouse. A to pick, B to go back.

First launch

The app isn't signed with an Apple developer certificate yet, so macOS asks you to OK it once.

  1. Install

    Open the DMG and drag Devious Licks into your Applications folder.

  2. Open it once

    Double-click Devious Licks. macOS says it can't check the app for malware. Click Done.

  3. Allow it

    Go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Devious Licks, then enter your Mac password.

You only do this once. After that, new versions arrive through the Update button in the app's Settings, so you won't need to download the DMG again.
